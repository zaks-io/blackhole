## NASA Deep Star Maps 2020

Files:

- `public/textures/starmap_2020_4k.exr`
- `public/textures/starmap_4k.webp`

The EXR is a recompressed copy of NASA's `starmap_2020_4k.exr`. The WebP is a tone-mapped and
compressed derivative of the same map.

- Source: [NASA Scientific Visualization Studio, Deep Star Maps 2020](https://svs.gsfc.nasa.gov/4851/)
- Terms: [NASA Images and Media Usage Guidelines](https://www.nasa.gov/nasa-brand-center/images-and-media/)
- Credit: NASA/Goddard Space Flight Center Scientific Visualization Studio. Gaia DR2:
  ESA/Gaia/DPAC.

The following project-rendered images incorporate the NASA star map as their background and are
therefore also subject to the NASA terms above:

- `public/blackhole-icon.webp`
- `public/blackhole-side-view.webp`
- `public/blackhole-warp.webp`

Files and originals:

- `public/textures/HDR_rich_blue_nebulae_1_4k.exr` derives from
  [`HDR_blue_nebulae_1.hdr`](https://www.spacespheremaps.com/wp-content/uploads/HDR_blue_nebulae_1.hdr).
- `public/textures/HDR_rich_multi_nebulae_2_4k.exr` derives from
  [`HDR_multi_nebulae_2.hdr`](https://www.spacespheremaps.com/wp-content/uploads/HDR_multi_nebulae_2.hdr).
- `public/textures/HDR_hazy_nebulae_4k.exr` derives from
  [`HDR_hazy_nebulae.hdr`](https://www.spacespheremaps.com/wp-content/uploads/HDR_hazy_nebulae.hdr).
- `public/textures/HDR_artificial_planet_4k.exr` derives from
  [`HDR_artificial_planet.hdr`](https://www.spacespheremaps.com/wp-content/uploads/HDR_artificial_planet.hdr).

The repository copies are resized and recompressed derivatives of the originals.

- Source: [Space Spheremaps HDRi Space Spheremaps](https://www.spacespheremaps.com/hdr-spheremaps/)

The source terms permit copying, redistribution, modification, and commercial use. They state
that attribution is appreciated but not required, and separately prohibit use of the images for
AI image scraping or training.
